Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how this platform collects, stores, uses, and protects the personal information of its users. We are committed to handling your data responsibly and transparently. This platform is an independent third-party service and is not affiliated with FIFA or any official tournament organiser.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use this platform:

— Full name
— Email address
— Phone or WhatsApp number
— Country of residence
— Booking preferences and history
— Payment-related details (once payment integration is active)
— Account login credentials

3. Automatically Collected Information

When you visit this platform, limited technical data may be collected automatically, including:

— Browser type and version
— Device type
— IP address
— Pages visited and time spent on the platform
— Interaction behaviour such as clicks, scrolling, and form use

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

— Process and manage your booking requests
— Provide customer support and respond to enquiries
— Personalise your experience on the platform
— Send booking confirmations and relevant updates
— Improve platform performance and user experience
— Protect against fraud, misuse, or unauthorised access

5. Data Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, misuse, disclosure, or loss. However, no online platform can provide absolute security, and we encourage users to keep their account credentials private and to contact us immediately if they suspect any unauthorised access.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. Information may be shared only where necessary for:

— Processing your bookings through third-party providers
— Legal compliance and regulatory requirements
— Fraud prevention and platform security
— Hosting and infrastructure management

7. Cookies

This platform may use cookies to improve functionality, remember your preferences, and analyse usage patterns. For more details on how cookies are used, please refer to our Cookie Policy.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

— Access the personal data we hold about you
— Request corrections to inaccurate information
— Request deletion of your data
— Withdraw consent for certain types of data processing
— Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To make any of the above requests, please contact us via the Support page, WhatsApp, or email.

9. International Users

This platform is accessible globally. World Cup 2026 fans from many different countries may use this service. By using the platform, you acknowledge that your data may be processed in the regions where our infrastructure is based. We aim to handle all user data in accordance with applicable privacy standards.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes to the platform, legal requirements, or service functionality. Continued use of the platform after any updates are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We recommend reviewing this page from time to time.

11. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your personal data is handled, please contact us via the Support page, WhatsApp, or email. We will respond to all privacy-related enquiries as promptly as possible.

Your Privacy is Important to Us

By using this platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you have any concerns or questions, our support team is available to help.
