Cookie Policy

How cookies and similar technologies may be used on this platform

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to remember your preferences, and to provide useful information to website owners about how users interact with their platform.

2. Why We Use Cookies

This platform may use cookies and similar technologies to:

— Remember your preferences and settings between visits
— Keep you signed in during your session
— Improve platform performance and loading speed
— Understand how users navigate and interact with the platform
— Support future booking and personalisation features

3. Types of Cookies We May Use

The platform may use the following types of cookies:

Essential Cookies

These are required for the platform to function correctly. Without them, certain features — such as account access and session management — may not work as intended. These cookies cannot be disabled without affecting core platform functionality.

Performance Cookies

These help us understand how visitors interact with the platform, which pages are most visited, and where improvements can be made. This data is used to improve the overall user experience and platform reliability.

Preference Cookies

These remember choices you have made — such as your language preference or region — so you do not need to re-select them on each visit. They help make your experience more consistent and convenient.

Analytics and Marketing Cookies

In the future, analytics tools or advertising platforms may be integrated into this platform. If so, associated cookies will be used in accordance with this policy and applicable privacy laws. We will update this section if and when such integrations are added.

4. Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Most modern browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies via their privacy or settings menus. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect how parts of this platform function, including your ability to stay signed in or access personalised features.

5. Effect of Disabling Cookies

If cookies are disabled, some platform features may not work correctly, including:

— Staying signed in between sessions
— Saved preferences and language settings
— Smoother navigation and page loading
— Booking-related session continuity

6. Third-Party Cookies

Some features integrated into this platform — such as payment tools, analytics services, or support widgets — may use their own cookies. These third-party cookies are governed by the privacy policies of those respective services, and we are not responsible for how they collect or use data.

7. Changes to This Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated from time to time as the platform evolves or new integrations are added. Any significant changes will be reflected on this page. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically to stay informed.

8. Questions About Cookies

If you have any questions about how cookies are used on this platform, please contact us through the Support page, WhatsApp, or email. We are happy to provide clarification on request.

Cookie Notice

By continuing to use this platform, you acknowledge that cookies and similar technologies may be used as described in this Cookie Policy. If you do not agree with the use of cookies, you may disable them through your browser settings, though this may affect your experience.
