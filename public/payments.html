Payments

Information about accepted payment methods and how the checkout process works

How Payments Work

Our platform is being set up to support multiple payment methods for ticket bookings, flights, hotels, and extras. The goal is to provide a straightforward, secure checkout experience for fans from all over the world.

Once payment integration is complete, you will be able to select your preferred method, review your booking details, and receive a confirmation after your transaction is processed. This platform acts as a booking intermediary and all transactions will be clearly displayed before confirmation.

Credit & Debit Cards

We plan to support Visa, Mastercard, and other major cards for straightforward and familiar checkout.

Includes: Visa, Mastercard, and other supported providers

Cryptocurrency

For fans who prefer digital payments, we intend to support cryptocurrency as a payment option through a secure and transparent process.

Includes: Bitcoin, Ethereum, USDT, and selected digital currencies

Digital Wallets

Wallet-based payment methods may be available to offer more flexibility at checkout, depending on your location and preference.

Includes: PayPal and similar options

Bank Transfer

Bank transfer may be available for selected high-value bookings or upon request through our support team.

Ideal for: Larger booking requests and assisted transactions

The Booking and Payment Process

1. Choose What You Need

Select the ticket, hotel, flight, or extra service you would like to book from the available listings on the platform.

2. Review Your Order

Check the details of your selection — including pricing, dates, and any applicable conditions — before proceeding to payment.

3. Complete Payment

Choose your payment method and complete the transaction through our secure checkout process. All pricing is displayed clearly before you confirm.

4. Receive Confirmation

Once your payment is confirmed, a booking reference and confirmation details will be available in your account and sent to your email.

Your Security Matters

We take payment security seriously. All transactions are handled with the aim of protecting customer data and financial information in line with standard security practices.

Supporting Global Fans

We understand that fans are travelling from many countries. Our payment options are being designed with international users in mind, including support for multiple currencies where possible.

Clear Pricing

We aim to display pricing transparently so you know exactly what you are paying for before completing any transaction. No hidden fees will be added without your knowledge.

Payment Options Coming Soon

Full payment integration is currently being finalised. More payment methods and a complete checkout flow will be available at launch. Contact support for assistance in the meantime.

Have a Payment Question?

If you have questions about payment methods, booking costs, or need assistance with a transaction, our support team is available via WhatsApp and email.
