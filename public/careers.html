Work With Us

We are building a useful, fan-focused travel platform and looking for people who share that goal

About the Team

We are a small but growing team working to make World Cup travel planning easier for fans around the world. The platform covers ticket search, flights, hotels, extras, customer support, and more — and we are always improving.

If you are interested in contributing — whether as a developer, designer, content writer, or support specialist — this page is for you. We value people who care about the user experience and who want to help build something genuinely useful.

What We Look For

Clear Thinking

We value people who can identify problems and work toward practical, user-focused solutions.

Honesty

We believe in transparent communication — with our users and within our team.

Global Perspective

Our users come from all over the world, and we want a team that reflects and understands that diversity.

Areas We May Hire In

Frontend Development

Building responsive, accessible, and user-friendly interfaces across booking, account, and support pages.

Backend Development

Working on APIs, booking logic, user authentication, data management, and platform performance.

Customer Support

Helping users with booking questions, account access, and general enquiries across WhatsApp, email, and live chat.

Content & Communications

Writing guides, FAQs, and platform content that is clear, accurate, and genuinely helpful to users.

Get in Touch

We are not always actively hiring, but we are open to hearing from people who are genuinely interested in what we are building.

If you would like to be considered for future roles or project collaborations, feel free to reach out via WhatsApp or email with a brief introduction and what you are interested in contributing.

Opportunities Will Be Listed Here

As the platform grows, any open roles or collaboration opportunities will be posted on this page. Check back periodically or get in touch directly.
