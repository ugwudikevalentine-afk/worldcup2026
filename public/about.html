About This Platform

An independent travel and booking assistance platform for World Cup 2026 fans

Welcome to the World Cup 2026 Travel Platform

FIFA World Cup 2026 is set to be one of the largest sporting events ever hosted, taking place across the United States, Canada, and Mexico, and featuring 48 national teams. For fans planning to attend, the logistics of travel, accommodation, and ticketing can be overwhelming.

This platform is designed to make that process easier. We bring together travel options, ticket listings, hotel searches, and extra services in one place — so fans can plan their World Cup experience without jumping between multiple websites.

About the 2026 Tournament

48 Teams

The 2026 tournament is the first FIFA World Cup to include 48 national teams, making it the most expansive edition in the competition's history.

3 Host Countries

The United States, Canada, and Mexico are jointly hosting the tournament, spanning three nations across North America.

16 Host Cities

Matches will be held across 16 cities, each offering a unique fan atmosphere and a range of travel and accommodation options.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to simplify World Cup travel planning for fans around the world. Whether you are travelling from a nearby city or flying internationally, finding the right combination of tickets, flights, and hotels can be time-consuming and confusing.

We aim to reduce that friction by bringing the most important booking options together in one platform — giving fans more time to focus on the experience itself. This is an independent service designed to assist with travel planning and booking convenience.
